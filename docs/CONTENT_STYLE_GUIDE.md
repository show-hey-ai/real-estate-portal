# Buyer guide style guide

How Welcome Home Tokyo writes buyer guides (`src/content/guides*.ts`). Based on a review on 2026-10-07 of note.com writers, SUUMO, LIFULL HOME'S PRESS, 楽待, 健美家, Housing Japan, Tokyo Portfolio, NerdWallet, Chinese-language Japan property guides, and Google's helpful-content guidance. We learn the techniques; we never copy text.

## Rules

1. **Answer first.** The intro answers the title's question in 2–3 sentences, then explains.
2. **Key takeaways near the top** (3–5 bullets) — already built into the template.
3. **Headings as the reader's questions** where natural (「初期費用はいつ、いくら必要？」 / "How much does it cost?").
4. **One worked example with real numbers per article**, labelled as illustrative (`example` on a section). Show the formula, and separate gross from net.
5. **Tables for comparisons and costs** (`table` on a section). Split money by when it is paid: contract, settlement, monthly.
6. **Who stands behind it.** Every guide shows the "About this guide" box with the licensed operator and licence number (`GuideReviewer`). Add a named 宅地建物取引士 only with a real name and consent.
7. **Dates that mean something.** Change `updatedAt` only when the content changes.
8. **Primary sources** (`sources`): 国税庁 タックスアンサー, 国土交通省, 法務省 and so on. Check every URL before adding it.
9. **First-hand experience.** Use real, anonymised cases from our own deals only. Never invent a case or figures that claim to be real.
10. **Mistake-avoidance angle**: name the common mistakes and how to check for them.
11. **Short paragraphs, checklists** for anything the reader must verify.
12. **Images that carry information** (timelines, maps, tables), not decoration.
13. **No promises of returns.** Under 宅建業法, no 断定的判断 about profits. Yields are calculations from stated figures, not forecasts.
14. **Scope and disclaimer**: say what the guide covers, and that it is not tax or legal advice for an individual case.
15. **One next step** at the end (listings, contact by LINE / WhatsApp / WeChat) plus related guides.

## Language versions

- **Japanese**: plain, polite tone; correct legal terms (重要事項説明, 管理規約); cost ranges in %, plus examples at a few price points; trust comes from experience together with supervision.
- **English**: scannable, answer first. Settle eligibility early ("non-residents can buy; no visa needed"). Gloss Japanese terms on first use, show JPY with USD where useful, and state the licence and a disclaimer.
- **Chinese**: early reassurance on ownership (土地所有權), buying remotely, total cost, net yield, resale tax, loans for foreigners, remittance and remote management. zh-TW: friendly and comparative, and LINE is common. zh-CN: data first, framed as asset allocation, and WeChat. Use regional vocabulary (管理費 vs 物业费 where appropriate).

## Template

1. Title (a question or a number), publish/updated dates, about box
2. 2–3 sentence answer (intro)
3. Key takeaways
4. 4–6 sections. Each opens with a one-line answer, then explanation, then a table or checklist where useful.
5. Worked example (illustrative)
6. Common mistakes / checklist
7. FAQ (3–5)
8. Sources
9. Next step and related guides
